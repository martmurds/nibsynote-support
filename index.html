Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my notes across devices?

NibsyNote syncs automatically through your iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID on each device and that iCloud Drive is enabled in Settings.

What's included in NibsyNote Pro?

Pro unlocks premium templates (Cornell, Dot Grid, Meeting, Client Meeting, Lecture, Workout, Habit Tracker, Storyboard, Music Staff), Apple Reminders priority sync, and advanced planning tools.

How do I restore a previous version of a note?

Open the note and tap the Time Travel scrubber to step back through earlier versions of the page.

I deleted a note by mistake — can I get it back?

Yes. Open Recently Deleted from the library to restore notes for up to 30 days after deletion.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings inside NibsyNote and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Privacy
