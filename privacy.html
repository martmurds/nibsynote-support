Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

NibsyNote ("the app") is designed to keep your notes private. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Information We Do Not Collect

NibsyNote does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Your Notes

All notes, priorities, schedules and attachments you create stay on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, your data is automatically synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. We never see or have access to your content. iCloud's handling of your data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple Reminders & Notifications

If you grant the app access to Apple Reminders, NibsyNote uses that access only to create reminders you have asked it to create. If you grant notification permission, the app schedules local notifications only for events and times you choose. We do not send remote push notifications carrying personal content.

Purchases

NibsyNote Pro is sold as an in-app subscription through Apple's App Store. Purchase transactions are handled by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment information. Apple's handling of your purchase data is governed by their privacy policy.

Children

NibsyNote does not knowingly collect any data, so it does not collect data from children under 13. The app is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
